Privacy & Ads

Last updated: July 2026

Texas Water Watchers (texaswaterwatchers.com) is a public visualization of groundwater, rainfall, and stream data for the Texas Hill Country, operated by Upward Axis Tech. We keep this simple: we don't ask you to create an account, and we don't sell your personal information.

What we collect

We use privacy-respecting analytics (Google Analytics and Contentsquare/Hotjar) to understand which pages are useful and how the site performs. These tools record standard, largely anonymous information such as pages viewed, approximate region, device and browser type, and how you move through the page. We do not use this to identify you personally.

Cookies & advertising

To help cover hosting costs, this site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and — where permitted — to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Google Ad Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info/choices.

If you're in the EU/UK, we present a consent choice before personalized ads are shown. Residents of California and similar jurisdictions may exercise applicable opt-out rights via the links above.

Data sources

Readings come from public agencies — the U.S. Geological Survey, the Texas Water Development Board, the Cow Creek Groundwater Conservation District, and the CoCoRaHS volunteer network via ACIS. This site is an independent presentation and is not affiliated with or endorsed by those agencies. The data is provisional and not a flood-warning or emergency tool — for warnings, see the National Weather Service.
